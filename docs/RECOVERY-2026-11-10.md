## Kiwify sales flow

Webhook receiver:

`https://hermes-pro-api-commercial.onrender.com/api/v1/webhooks/kiwify`

Configure the same random token in Render as `KIWIFY_WEBHOOK_TOKEN` and in the Kiwify webhook's Token field. Do not put the token in the URL. Kiwify sends `?signature=` and Hermes verifies HMAC-SHA1 against the raw request body.

Suggested events: purchase approved, rejected, refunded, chargeback, Pix generated, boleto generated, and subscription lifecycle events when subscriptions are sold.

Hermes can save a Kiwify checkout URL on an ebook in `ready_to_publish`. Creating the Kiwify product, setting its price, and uploading/configuring delivery still happen in the Kiwify dashboard. Checkout saved in Hermes is not the same as a purchase verified. The Sales screen must only show persisted Kiwify/Hotmart events; revenue is not profit.
